About the convener

Meet Femi Maven.

Founder, Maven Mint Academy

Oluwafemi Oyenuga, professionally known as Femi Maven and widely called “The King of Value,” is an accomplished motion graphics designer, creative educator, public speaker, and founder. He has worked with brands across fintech, SaaS, and other sectors, and founded Maven Mint, a motion design and animation academy that has trained thousands of creatives.

WIMD was created from that same desire: to help more women discover what is possible for them in motion design.

Frequently asked questions

Got questions?

01What’s WIMD all about?+

Women in Motion Design is a movement created to empower the next generation of African women to thrive in motion design. We’re breaking barriers and creating a community where women can learn, connect, grow, create, and confidently take their place in the industry.

02Who is WIMD for?+

WIMD is for women at every stage — beginner, student, freelancer, professional or business owner. There’s a place for you here.

03Do I have to be a motion designer to join?+

No. You don’t have to be an expert or professional motion designer. If you’re interested in motion design and want to learn, grow and connect, you’re welcome.

04Is WIMD only for women?+

Yes. WIMD is exclusively for women and was created specifically to give women a dedicated space to learn, connect, grow and explore opportunities in motion design.

05When is the WIMD Summit?+

October 19 – November 2, 2026. That’s 14 days of learning, connection, practical training and growth.

06Is the summit virtual or physical?+

The first edition is fully virtual. You can participate from wherever you are without needing to travel. Speaker sessions will be on Google Meet and practical training on Telegram.

07Is joining the waitlist free?+

Yes. Joining the waitlist is completely free. The waitlist closes on September 25, 2026. Ticket registration opens September 26, 2026 at 9:00 AM and closes October 18, 2026.

08Will the sessions be recorded?+

Yes. Recordings will be available after the summit exclusively to women who purchase a summit ticket. Those who do not purchase a ticket can access recordings later for a separate fee.

09What apps/software will we learn?+

You’ll learn with Alight Motion, Adobe After Effects and CapCut — with training around both mobile and PC approaches.

10Can someone who uses a PC register?+

Absolutely. The summit supports women interested in both mobile and PC motion design.
