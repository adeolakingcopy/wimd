About the Summit

Empowering the next generation of African women to thrive in motion design.

The Women in Motion Design Summit is a free virtual event created to inspire, equip, and empower the next generation of African women to build successful careers in motion design.

Bringing together leading professionals, inspiring speakers, and ambitious women from across the continent, the summit provides a unique opportunity to learn industry-relevant skills, gain valuable career insights, and connect with a supportive community of creatives.

Whether you're a student, stay-at-home mum, entrepreneur, freelancer, or someone looking to learn a valuable digital skill, this summit is designed to help you take your first step into motion design. For two weeks, you'll learn from experienced motion designers and discover industry opportunities.

This is more than an event—it's the beginning of a movement to raise a new generation of African women who will create, innovate, lead, and shape the future of motion design.

04 / Convener

Meet the Convener

Femi Maven is an award-winning motion graphics designer, creative educator, and founder of Maven Mint Academy.

With years of experience in motion design, Femi has collaborated with startups, fintech companies, SaaS brands, and businesses across various industries, delivering engaging visual experiences that help brands communicate their stories effectively.

Passionate about developing the next generation of creative professionals, he has built one of Africa's growing motion design communities through Maven Mint, empowering over 50,000 creatives through training programs, workshops, and educational initiatives.

In recognition of his impact on the creative industry, Femi received the Africa Media Excellence Award at the Gen Z Awards. He is also a respected public speaker, committed to inspiring, mentoring, and equipping creatives with the skills, mindset, and opportunities they need to build successful careers.

As the convener of the Women in Motion Design Summit, Femi's vision is to empower the next generation of African women with the knowledge, confidence, and community to thrive in the motion design industry.
