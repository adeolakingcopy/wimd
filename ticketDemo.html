07 / FAQ

Good questions.

Who is the summit for?

African women who are curious about motion design, learning the craft, or ready to grow a creative career—from complete beginners to working creatives.

Is it completely virtual?

Yes. The summit is fully virtual, so you can join from wherever you are.

How long does it run?

The summit runs for 14 days, from October 19 to November 2, 2026.

How do I access the sessions?

Access instructions and the full schedule will be sent to registered attendees before the summit begins.

Will the sessions be recorded?

Recording access has not been announced yet. Ticket holders will receive confirmed access details directly.

What experience level do I need?

None. You can join as a beginner, a self-taught creative, or an experienced designer looking for perspective and community.

Do I need to be a woman to attend?

The summit is designed to advance African women in motion design. Any additional attendance guidance will be shared during registration.
