About the Summit

Empowering the next generation of African women to thrive in motion design.

The Women in Motion Design Summit is a free virtual event created to inspire, equip, and empower the next generation of African women to build successful careers in motion design.

Bringing together leading professionals, inspiring speakers, and ambitious women from across the continent, the summit provides a unique opportunity to learn industry-relevant skills, gain valuable career insights, and connect with a supportive community of creatives.

Whether you're a student, stay-at-home mum, entrepreneur, freelancer, or someone looking to learn a valuable digital skill, this summit is designed to help you take your first step into motion design.

This is more than an event, it's the beginning of a movement to raise a new generation of African women who will create, innovate, lead, and shape the future of motion design.

What You'll Learn

Here's what we'll cover throughout the 14-day summit.

The Women in Motion Design Summit is designed to give you both the mindset and the practical skills needed to build a successful career in motion design.

WEEK 1

Learn from Industry Leaders

Gain valuable insights from four accomplished women who have built successful careers in motion design. You'll learn how to:

  • Break into the motion design industry as a woman
  • Build a successful and sustainable career
  • Understand the business side and how to earn from your skills
  • Build meaningful relationships through networking and collaboration
  • Develop the mindset and habits of top-performing designers
  • Create exceptional work that attracts brands and opportunities
  • Navigate challenges and grow with confidence
WEEK 2

Practical Motion Design Training

Learn directly from experienced instructors through hands-on training create professional projects on a smartphone, and professional motion graphics using Adobe After Effects on a PC. You'll learn how to:

  • Create engaging promotional videos
  • Design compelling explainer videos
  • Animate modern user interface (UI) designs
  • Create storyboards and styleframes for animation projects
  • Build a professional portfolio that attracts clients and employers
  • Collaborate effectively with other creatives
  • Apply industry best practices to your creative workflow

Motion Design Challenge

At the end of the training, participants will take part in a motion design challenge giving them the opportunity to apply everything they've learned, showcase their creativity, receive feedback, and compete for exciting prizes.

Meet the Convener

Femi Maven

Award-winning motion graphics designer, creative educator, and founder of Maven Mint Academy.

With years of experience in motion design, Femi has collaborated with startups, fintech companies, SaaS brands, and businesses across various industries, delivering engaging visual experiences that help brands communicate their stories effectively.

Passionate about developing the next generation of creative professionals, he has built one of Africa's growing motion design communities through Maven Mint, empowering over 50,000 creatives through training programs, workshops, and educational initiatives.

He is also a respected public speaker, committed to inspiring, mentoring, and equipping creatives with the skills, mindset, and opportunities they need to build successful careers.

As the convener of the Women in Motion Design Summit, Femi's vision is to empower the next generation of African women with the knowledge, confidence, and community to thrive in the motion design industry.
