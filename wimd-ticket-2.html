This Is More Than a Summit.

WIMD is a space created for women who want to learn, grow, create, connect, and take their place in the motion design industry.

For 14 days, you'll learn from 8 industry experts and 3 expert instructors, participate in practical activities and challenges, connect with other women, and gain access to resources designed to help you grow your skills and explore opportunities in motion design.

What will you learn?

Two Weeks. One Transformation.

The WIMD Summit is designed across two weeks, with the first week focused on industry conversations and expert insights, followed by a week of practical training.

Week 1

Learn from Industry Experts

Throughout the first week, you'll hear from women with real experience across motion design, creative business, personal branding, visibility, networking, confidence, and career growth.

Week 2

Practical Training

The second week is focused on practical learning. Across seven days, participants will learn design fundamentals, motion design, promotional and explainer video creation, Adobe After Effects, SaaS video production, and how to position themselves for opportunities in the industry.

All practical training sessions hold from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM on Telegram.

Learn. Create. Practice. Grow.

Week 2 is designed to take participants beyond simply watching and listening. You'll learn practical skills, follow real creative processes, and gain knowledge you can begin applying to your own projects.

Come ready to learn, create, ask questions, and put what you learn into practice.

The WIMD Motion Design Challenge

Create. Submit. Win.

At the end of the summit, you'll get a real creative brief and apply what you've learned to create a motion design piece for a brand. You'll also have opportunities to win exciting prizes — including brand-new phones — and to earn Maven Mint Academy scholarships.

You don't have to own expensive equipment to begin your journey. Start with what you have — Alight Motion on your phone, CapCut, or Adobe After Effects. Learn. Practice. Grow.

About the Convener

Femi Maven

Founder, Maven Mint Academy

Oluwafemi Oyenuga, professionally known as Femi Maven and widely called “The King of Value,” is an accomplished motion graphics designer, creative educator, public speaker, and founder.

With years of experience in the creative industry, he has worked with brands across fintech, SaaS, and other sectors, delivering motion design and visual storytelling projects for both local and international companies. He has been recognized for his contributions to the creative industry, including receiving the Africa Media Excellence Award at the Gen Z Awards.

He is also the founder of Maven Mint, a motion design and animation academy that has trained thousands of creatives through courses, programs, events, and community initiatives. WIMD was created from that same desire: to help more women discover what is possible for them in motion design.
